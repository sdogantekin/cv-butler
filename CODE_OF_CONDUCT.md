# Code of Conduct

CV Butler is a project for people looking for work, and many contributors and users are in a stressful moment. We want participation to be respectful and welcoming.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and non-native English speakers.
- Give and accept feedback on ideas and code, not on people.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.
- Respect privacy. Never post real resumes, personal data, or credentials in issues or pull requests; redact or use fictional examples.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or spam.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues, and contributions, and may block anyone who breaks these rules. To report a concern, contact the maintainer through the options on their GitHub profile at [github.com/sdogantekin](https://github.com/sdogantekin). Reports are handled privately.

This code applies in all project spaces, such as the repository, issues, pull requests, and discussions.
